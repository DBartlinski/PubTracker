How these numbers are pulled and how to read them

Where the numbers come from

  • Dimensions (the baseline): VA-affiliated publications from the VA Dimensions export for fiscal year 2026 (Oct 1, 2025 – Sep 30, 2026). Dimensions is treated as the complete list; every publication entered in PubTracker should also be in Dimensions, but not the other way around.
  • What counts: journal publications only. Conference abstracts, corrections, preprints, book chapters, proceedings, books, monographs and seminars are left out, as are records with only a publication year and no exact date (those cannot be placed in a quarter).
  • Dates: each publication is placed in a fiscal quarter by its Dimensions publication date (print date, then online date, then general publication date). PubTracker's submission and publication dates are never used, because they are often missing or inaccurate.
  • PubTracker: all submissions of type publication from the 2025 and 2026 PubTracker exports (pulled Oct 5, 2026).
  • Facility: a Dimensions publication is assigned to a VA facility from the organization names in its author affiliations. National umbrella organizations (VA Central Office / station 101) are not counted as a facility.
  • Funding: ORD funded, VA grant codes and ORD portfolio are detected from VA award prefixes and service names in the Dimensions funding and acknowledgement text (CX = CSR&D, BX = BLR&D, RX = RR&D, HX = HSR&D, plus QUERI and CSP). Funders and Grant numbers are the funders and awards Dimensions lists.

How publications are matched to PubTracker

The two systems share no DOI or PubMed ID, so publications are matched by title. Titles are lower-cased and stripped of punctuation first. An identical title is a match. Otherwise a PubTracker title that is at least 90% similar also counts, but only if the facilities agree (same facility, or one side has no facility). A Dimensions publication with no match is counted as not in PubTracker.

Reading the results

  • Compliance % = publications entered in PubTracker ÷ Dimensions publications for the chosen period. Not in PubTracker + Entered in PubTracker always equals Dimensions publications.
  • Period: pick the whole year or one quarter. A publication belongs to the quarter of its Dimensions date and counts as entered if any PubTracker submission matches it, whenever that submission was made.
  • Compliance by facility: sorted by the most publications not in PubTracker. A publication with several facilities counts toward each one, so facility rows add up to more than the overall total.
  • Unattributed (no VAMC match) holds publications whose affiliations did not map to a known facility. They count in the overall total but belong to no facility.
  • Publication drill-down: click facilities in the table to narrow the list, then filter by funding, ORD portfolio or search text. Switch between Not in PubTracker and In PubTracker. Click a row for details; the download includes every row that matches your selection.
  • Funding filter: ORD-funded narrows the list to publications with VA grant evidence, usually the ones most expected to be in PubTracker.

Keep in mind: Dimensions lists all VA-affiliated publications, not only those required in PubTracker, so a low rate shows a gap to look into, not proof of non-compliance. Matching is by title only, so a paper whose title changed between submission and publication can be missed. Facility and funding tags are inferred from text and can be incomplete. Check the listed publications before acting on them.
