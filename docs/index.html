Dimensions-PubTracker Complience Oct 2026

What this page shows. It treats VA Dimensions as the complete record of VA-affiliated publications and PubTracker as the user-submitted supplement being checked. For each facility it reports how many Dimensions publications also appear in PubTracker (the submission rate) and lists the ones that do not, so you can see what may be missing from PubTracker.
How the data is used and how to read it

Where the numbers come from

  • Dimensions: VA-affiliated publications from the VA Dimensions export, for fiscal year 2026 (Oct 1, 2025 – Sep 30, 2026). Conference abstracts and corrections are left out, as are records with only a publication year and no exact date. Each record is placed in a fiscal quarter by its publication date.
  • PubTracker: submissions of type publication from the 2025 and 2026 PubTracker exports (pulled Oct 5, 2026). Each is dated by its Publication Date, or its Date Created when that is blank, and is compared only with Dimensions records from the same period you select.
  • Facility: a Dimensions record is assigned to a facility from the organization names in its affiliations. A PubTracker submission is assigned from its point-of-contact station number.

How records are matched

The two systems share no ID, so records are matched by title. Titles are lower-cased and stripped of punctuation first. An identical title is an exact match. Otherwise the closest PubTracker title counts as a fuzzy match if its similarity is at or above the slider value and the facilities agree (the same facility, or one side has no facility). A Dimensions record with no match is counted as missing.

The slider

  • 100% allows exact matches only. This is the strictest setting: fewest records found, most missing.
  • Lower values also accept near-identical titles, such as different punctuation or a small wording change. More records are found, but the chance of a wrong match grows. The default is 90%, and the minimum is 70%.
  • Watch the Exact / fuzzy card: the exact count never changes with the slider, so the fuzzy count shows how much the slider is adding. If a small move changes the results a lot, review the borderline titles by hand.

Reading the results

  • Overall submission rate = records found in PubTracker ÷ Dimensions records for the period.
  • Facility table: sorted by most missing records. Submission rate is that facility's found ÷ total. Click a row to list its missing records, with DOI, PubMed and Dimensions links, and download them as CSV.
  • A publication with several facilities counts toward each one, so facility rows add up to more than the overall total.
  • Unattributed holds records whose affiliations did not map to a known facility. They count in the overall total but belong to no facility.
  • Period: choose the whole year or one quarter. Records are tied to a period by publication date, so a PubTracker entry whose date falls in a different quarter than its Dimensions record can show as missing in a quarter view.

Keep in mind: Dimensions lists all VA-affiliated publications, not only those required in PubTracker, so a low rate shows a gap to investigate, not proof of non-compliance. Matching is by title only, so a retitled paper can be missed. Check the listed records before acting on them.
