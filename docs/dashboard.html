About this data: Records are exported from Dimensions (third-party bibliometric database), filtered to the current VA fiscal year and VA-affiliated orgs. Conference abstracts/corrections are excluded by default. Facility matches are name-based text evidence, not authoritative. ORD portfolio tags are inferred estimates, not official VA data. Citation/RCR/FCR reflect Dimensions' data as of export time. Treat all figures as directional, for internal review only.

Dimensions has no dedicated ORD field, so Broad Portfolio tags are inferred from VA grant award prefixes and service names in funding/acknowledgement text. Actively Managed Portfolio tags are inferred from topic keywords in title/abstract/MeSH terms, but a topic match alone is not funding evidence — an Actively Managed tag is only counted here once the record also carries ORD funding evidence (a Broad Portfolio grant/service, or the portfolio's own name in the funding text). Topic-only matches with no funding evidence are excluded from these totals. Treat all figures as estimates, not an authoritative VA source of truth; PubTracker's own service field is not used as a cross-check because it only covers user-submitted records and is not comprehensive.

How to read these five numbers
Publications
Total records passing the current filters — the denominator for the four percentages below.
Total ORD-funded (unique)
The deduplicated count of publications with any ORD funding evidence — Broad Portfolio evidence, an Actively Managed tag, or both. Use this number, not the sum of the two categories below, as the true funded total; summing them would double-count publications that carry both tags.
With Broad Portfolio evidence
Publications where we found a VA grant award prefix (e.g., CX, BX, RX, HX) or an explicit VA research-service name (e.g., "Health Systems Research") in the funding or acknowledgement text.
With Actively Managed tag
Publications where the title, abstract, or MeSH terms matched keywords for one of the six Actively Managed Portfolios (e.g., "traumatic brain injury," "suicide prevention") and the record also has ORD funding evidence. Publications that only match the topic with no funding evidence are not counted here — a paper can discuss a topic like suicide prevention with no VA funding tie at all, so a topic match alone is not treated as ORD-funded.
No ORD signal detected
Publications with no ORD funding evidence at all. This is expected to be the majority of records: most publications in this dataset simply have a VA-affiliated author and are not VA-funded research, so there is nothing in the text for our detector to find. A high count here is normal, not a data quality problem.

Interpretation guidance: A publication can have both a Broad Portfolio and an Actively Managed tag or only one — the two "with" categories are not mutually exclusive and will not sum to "Total ORD-funded." Use Broad Portfolio numbers when you need a defensible funding-service estimate; Actively Managed numbers are a narrower view of which funded publications also match a specific cross-cutting topic.

Broad Portfolio (VA funding service)

Count of publications with each Broad Portfolio tag. Bars can overlap (a publication with two tags counts in two bars), so bars will not sum to "With Broad Portfolio evidence" above.

Actively Managed Portfolio (topic)

Count of publications matching each Actively Managed Portfolio's keywords. Same overlap caveat applies — these are topic matches, not confirmed funding.

Broad Portfolio by facility

For publications with both a facility match and a Broad Portfolio tag, counts how many publications link each facility to each portfolio. A publication with multiple facilities or portfolios contributes to multiple rows.

Metrics are computed by Dimensions, not calculated locally, across the currently filtered records. Times cited = raw citation count at export time. RCR/FCR = field-normalized citation scores (1.0 = average for that field). Citation counts naturally grow with publication age — use for context, not to rank research quality.

What do these numbers mean?
Median citations
The middle value of "Times cited" across filtered publications; half have more citations, half have fewer. Median (not mean) is used here because a few highly-cited papers can otherwise skew the average.
Mean RCR
Average Relative Citation Ratio. 1.0 = typical citation performance for the paper's field and age; above 1.0 is above-average, below 1.0 is below-average. "N/A" means no filtered records have an RCR value.
Mean FCR
Average Field Citation Ratio, Dimensions' own field-normalized measure, interpreted the same way as RCR (1.0 = average).
Unique journals
Count of distinct journal/source titles among the filtered publications.
Top journals

The 15 journals publishing the most filtered records — a measure of where VA-affiliated research appears, not of journal quality.

Citation distribution

How many publications fall into each citation-count bucket. Citation counts are capped for display at 100+; newer publications will naturally cluster at the low end regardless of eventual impact.
